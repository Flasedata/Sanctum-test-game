CODEX OF THE FIRSTS · HOW TO PLAY

01

🗺️ THE MAP & PROCEDURAL REALM EXPANSION

Your mission is to protect the Ancient Sanctum (20 HP) from the alien fleet marching along the sunken cobblestone path.

Waves 1-10 (Starting Island): Defend the Ancient Sanctum against waves of scouts, troopers, and shielders. Earn 💎 Shards on every kill.
Wave 10 Boss Fight: Vanquish the colossal Astral Leviathan. Defeating bosses drops 🔮 Astral Essence!
Wave 10 Realm Expansion: Choose any edge arrow (North, East, South, West) to seamlessly generate a new 16x16 sector with animated rising voxels, newly connected paths, and active portals!
02

🗿 THE 4 PRIMAL GOLEMS & 5-TIER ASCENDANCE

Summon Golems onto Rune Pedestals along the path. Upgrade them up to Level 5 to unlock visual 3D voxel armor evolutions, Tier 3 Capstones, and Level 5 Primal Ascendance perks.

03

🔮 ELEMENTAL INFUSIONS & COMBO REACTIONS

Defeat bosses to gather 🔮 Astral Essence. Spend 1 Essence in the Golem Inspector to permanently infuse a Golem with an elemental affinity.

⚡ THUNDER

Applies Zapped status. Shocks enemies every 1.0s and inflicts periodic stuns.

🌍 EARTH

+30% Damage, physics knockback impulse (2.2s anti-pinning cooldown), and Dirty slow.

🔥 FIRE

+50% Damage. Inflicts Burned dealing 2% max HP/s continuous fire damage.

💧 WATER

Adds AoE Splash to single-target attacks and applies Wet status to invaders.

💥 ELEMENTAL COMBO REACTIONS

💥 COMBUSTION EXPLOSION
🔥 FIRE + 💧 WET

A direct Fire hit on a Wet enemy causes an instant localized detonation dealing massive area damage!

🌿💧 MUD SLOWDOWN
🌍 DIRTY + 💧 WET

Moistened earth thickens into heavy mud, slashing enemy movement speed by an overwhelming 50%!

⚡💧 2x ELECTRO-SHOCK
⚡ THUNDER + 💧 WET

Water conducts electricity! Thunder attacks deal a staggering 200% double damage against Wet enemies.

🌪️ VOXEL TORNADO
⚡ + 🌍 + 🔥 + 💧 ALL 4 ELEMENTS

Applying all four elemental statuses simultaneously unleashes a catastrophic vortex that sweeps enemies backward!

04

👾 THE ALIEN INVADERS & ELITES

The alien armada is comprised of specialized units, endgame elite variants, and dreadnought leviathans.

🟣 Void Scout
Speed: 1.25 (Fast) · HP: 45 · Reward: 8 💎

Agile airborne reconnaissance units that dash towards the Sanctum. Vulnerable to fast Oak snipers.

👾 Void Trooper
Speed: 0.80 · HP: 110 · Reward: 15 💎

Standard frontline shock infantry armed with scythe blades. Sustained damage from Stone & Brick is effective.

🛡️ Phase Shielder
Speed: 0.65 · HP: 240 · Reward: 25 💎

Heavy bulwark absorbing 50% of Physical damage. Vulnerable to Armor Shred (Brick) and Magic damage (Diorite)!

🕷️ Swarm Skitterer
Speed: 1.45 (Very Fast) · HP: 35 · Reward: 6 💎

Fast quadruped pack skirmishers attacking in dense bursts every 0.25s. Stone & Brick AoE annihilates them.

💀 Astral Leviathan (Boss)
Speed: 0.64 · Base HP: 2200 · Reward: 220 💎 + 1 🔮

Colossal dreadnought. Emits an AoE speed-boosting aura every 4.0s to nearby allies. 20% all-damage resist. Spawns dual bosses on Wave 15!

⭐ UNIQUE SHINY VARIANTS & STATUS RESISTANCES: Starting at Wave 5, non-boss enemies have a chance to spawn with golden crowns and special auras (Titan HP, Swift Speed, Life Regen, Warding). Starting at Wave 11, enemies develop Stun/Slow resistances (capped at 65%) and rare immunity traits (STUN IMMUNE / SLOW IMMUNE).
05

🎮 KEYBINDINGS & TACTICAL SHORTCUTS

Master the command controls to maintain complete tactical dominance over the battlefield.

[ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ 3 ] [ 4 ] Select Golem to build (Stone, Oak, Brick, Diorite).
[ CLICK PEDESTAL ] Summon the selected Golem onto an empty Rune Pedestal.
[ CLICK GOLEM ] Open the Contextual Inspector: Upgrade, Ascend, Infuse Elements, and select 4-Grid Priority (FIRST, LAST, STRONG, CLOSE).
[ SPACEBAR ] Start the next invasion wave immediately.
[ SPEED 1x / 2x / 4x ] Toggle between Normal, Double, and Quadruple simulation speed.
[ DRAG MOUSE ] Pan the isometric camera across the expanded realm.
[ MOUSE WHEEL ] Zoom the camera in for close-up inspection or out for wide tactical overview.
